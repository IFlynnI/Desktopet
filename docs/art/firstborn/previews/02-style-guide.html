firstborn: gate 2, Brim's style guide, version 2

The rules are in 02-style-guide.md. Version 1 is kept in history/style-v1/.

What changed. Brim is now a tiny 3D model drawn as pixel art. Every part is a real 3D shape seen by one fixed camera: turned 28° towards you and looking down 12°. Each pixel casts one ray, and the first shape it hits gives the pixel its part and its shade. Then the same pixel-art rules apply as before, and hand cleanup still goes on top. This is what fixes your four points below, so each one is shown against version 1.

Your four points, version 1 against version 2

1. The far horn, far wing and far foot now sit towards the snout side and a little higher, partly hidden. The near ones sit towards the back and lower. The feet stand close together under the body, not spread out like a side view.

1. Perspective: one camera for every part

2. The tail, the thighs and the belly are one soft surface now, so their shading flows across the joins and there's no seam line between them. The wing is a membrane lying on the back, rooted at the shoulder blade.

2a. Where the tail grows out of the body

2b. Where the wing roots in the back

2c. Where the legs meet the body

3. The flame is 7 × 10 px instead of 4 × 5, drawn by hand in four flicker Frames.

3. The tail flame

Its four flicker Frames

4. The helmet acts, and now it sits on the head

The helmet is a real shell with an inside. It rocks about the head's centre, so it slides over the skull instead of floating above it or cutting into it. Tipped back, it shows its lilac inside. Slipped forward, its brow comes down over the eyes. The horns always pass through slots in it.
