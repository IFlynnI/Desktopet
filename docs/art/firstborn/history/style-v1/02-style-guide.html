Body: the Form's rectangle, from the dome to the feet. Emotes sit above its top, so they clear the helmet.

Anchor: where the feet meet the Ground plane. The dotted line is the axis the art mirrors about.

Brim stands 53 px tall to the horn tips and 46 px wide with the tail. The head and helmet are 55 % of the height: a chibi build that is cute and top-heavy, so the helmet is kept balanced over the feet in every pose. The 8 rows above the horns are headroom for the hatch.
